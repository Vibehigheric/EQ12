CNET Shopping collects the following information that could potentially personally identify you:

  • IP address, which we use to determine your geographic location so that we can calculate accurate shipping rates
  • The URLs of the pages you visit on websites where our price comparison and coupons features are active. We use this information to offer price comparisons and coupons relevant to the page you are viewing.

CNET Shopping collects the following technical information:

  • Browser type, operating system, and interactions with the CNET Shopping Add-On, which we use to improve our products.

Full details about the data we collect, what we do with it, and your data rights are available in our Privacy Policy.
