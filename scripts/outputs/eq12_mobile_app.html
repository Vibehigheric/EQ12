Here’s a mobile-first UX/UI blueprint for EQ12, a premium NHL parlay app. It focuses on touch-first interactions, live odds, fast parlay building, and a distinct hockey identity. Brand and feel - Brand: EQ12 (logomark: stylized puck “Q” with a subtle ice texture) - Visual style: premium, clean, high-contrast text over subtle ice-rink gradients; soft glassmorphism on cards - Motion: smooth, spring-based micro-interactions; subtle parallax on background ice patterns - Sound (optional, toggleable): soft puck “clack” for add-to-slip, whistle for odds updates, crowd swell on bet placed Navigation and structure (bottom navigation bar) - Home: Live games, quick picks, trending parlays - Build: Quick Bet Slip Builder - Favorites: Saved parlays and followed teams/games - Notifications: Push settings and alerts center - Account: Profile, payments, preferences, responsible gaming Mobile-first patterns - Large, thumb-friendly buttons and chips (min 44–48dp touch targets) - Swipeable parlay cards: swipe left to save/share, right to add/remove legs - Collapsible sections for game slates, markets, and slip details - Pull-to-refresh on all feeds with a rink-line progress indicator - Sticky bottom “Bet Slip” tray with leg count and estimated payout, expandable with an upward swipe Screens and key features 1) Home - Header: EQ12 logo, search icon, notification bell with dot for unread - Ice rink background: faint blue/white rink lines, parallax scroll - Live Now (collapsible): horizontally scrollable cards with: - Team badges and dynamic team-color accents - Moneyline, puck line, total chips - Animated odds: green pulse up for improved odds, red pulse down for worse - Tonight’s Matchups (collapsible): upcoming games with quick add chips for common markets - Trending Parlays (swipeable): - Card shows legs summary, implied payout, community saves - Swipe actions: left to Save or Share; right to Expand or Add to Slip - Quick Actions bar: Build Parlay, My Favorites, Boosted Odds (if applicable) - Pull-to-refresh: stick + puck spinner animating along a center-ice faceoff dot 2) Build (Quick Bet Slip Builder) - Top tabs: Moneyline, Puck Line, Totals, Player Props (e.g., Shots on Goal, Points) - Grid of matchups with one-tap market chips - Quick Add interactions: - Tap to add a leg (chip turns filled; haptic tick; puck “clack” sound) - Long-press a leg to open alternate lines/props in a bottom sheet - Filters: Team, Time, Favorites, Boosted only - Selected legs bar: sticky at bottom; shows count and fractional/decimal/US odds toggle - “Review Slip” CTA animates to open slip sheet 3) Bet Slip (expandable tray) - Collapsible header: Parlay name (auto-generated, editable), legs count - Legs list: - Each leg tile shows team colors, market, odds, and an animated “last updated” ping - Swipe left to remove leg; reorder by long-press drag for clarity - Stake input: numeric keypad, presets (5, 10, 25, 50), “Max” - Payout module: estimated payout with animated change on odds updates - Price change guard: if odds change on submit, show confirmation dialog with updated odds - Place Bet button: large gradient button with micro-press animation and a brief confirm haptic - Save Parlay button: bookmark to Favorites for later - Share: social share sheet with a branded card (team logos, legs, and deep link) 4) Favorites - Saved Parlays: list of cards with last odds refresh time - Followed Teams/Games: quick tiles to jump into matchups - Bulk actions: refresh odds for all saved parlays, share multiple - Empty state: puck and stick icon with “Save your favorite parlays to come back fast” 5) Notifications - Global toggle: Enable Push Notifications - Categories: - Live game alerts: goals, penalties, period start/end - Odds changes: for saved parlays, watched markets - Bet status: placed, settled, cash-out opportunities (if applicable) - Promotions/boosts (optional) - Quiet hours and frequency settings - Per-team/per-parlay toggles and thresholds (e.g., alert only if odds move > 10%) 6) Account - Profile, payment methods, transaction history - Preferences: odds format, units, theme sounds, haptics, data saver - Responsible gaming tools: limits, cool-off, support links - Legal/compliance info Hockey theme integration - Backgrounds: subtle ice textures with blue and red line patterns; dark mode uses slate/graphite with frosted overlays - Icons: stick, puck, goal lamp, rink lines; simple, duotone style - Team colors: accent chips and progress bars adapt to selected teams; ensure accessible contrast with overlays - Microcopy: “Face off with EQ12,” “Hat trick picks,” “Power play boosts” Core components and interactions Parlay Card (swipeable) - Default state: legs count, payout estimate, save count, share icon - Swipe left: - Short: Save - Long: Share (reveals share actions) - Swipe right: - Short: Expand details - Long: Add legs to slip - Expanded details: list of legs, odds breakdown, “Load Variations” to swap a leg quickly Odds Chip with live updates - States: default, improving (green up arrow + light pulse), worsening (red down arrow + fade) - Transition timing: 150–250ms pulse; update label count-up/down for fractional changes Collapsible Sections - Header with arrow chevron that rotates 0–180° - Remember last state per user; auto-collapse non-active sections to keep screens short Pull-to-refresh - Rink spinner: puck slides along a curved path; on release, stick “taps” puck to trigger refresh - Haptic light tick on complete; confetti of tiny ice particles for significant updates (optional) Bottom Navigation Bar - Icons: Home (goal lamp), Build (sticks crossed), Favorites (star/puck), Notifs (bell), Account (jersey) - Active tab has a glow accent in team color when relevant (contextual) Animations and haptics - Add to slip: scale-up on chip, drop-in animation into slip tray, light haptic - Remove leg: swipe-off with subtle ice “skid” - Place bet: progress ring around button; success checkmark with short crowd swell sound - Microdurations: 150–250ms for small transitions; 300–400ms for modal/bottom sheets Design system (tokens) - Colors - Ice White #F7FAFF - Rink Blue #2F6DAE - Center Red #D84444 - Boards Grey #101317 (dark), #E9EDF5 (light surfaces) - Puck Black #0B0C0E - Accent Green (odds up) #2BC569 - Accent Red (odds down) #FF5A5F - Typography - Display: SF Pro / Inter Semibold - Body: SF Pro / Inter Regular - Numeric: Tabular figures for odds/payouts - Elevation - Frosted glass cards with blur 12–16, subtle shadow; hover-like highlight on press - Spacing - 8pt system; minimum 16pt padding on edges - Iconography - 24px default, 28–32px in bottom nav Accessibility - WCAG AA contrast; avoid relying on green/red only—use arrows and patterns - Dynamic type support; responsive layouts for L/XL text - VoiceOver labels for odds changes (“Odds improved from +120 to +130”) - Large target areas; focus order logical in bottom sheets Data and updates - Live odds via WebSocket; fallback to short polling - Client state model - Game: id, teams, startTime, status, scores - Market: id, type (ML, PL, Total, Prop), line, odds, lastUpdated - Leg: gameId, marketId, selection, odds, line - Parlay: id, legs[], combinedOdds, estPayout, saved, sharedCount, lastUpdated - Update strategy - Diff incoming odds; animate only when numeric value changes - Debounce rapid updates to 300–500ms - Show “price changed” confirmation on submit Social sharing - Share card: team logos, leg list, EQ12 branding, short URL deep link to rebuild the parlay - Share targets: Messages, X/Twitter, Instagram story (export image), clipboard link Favorites and saved lists - Save individual parlays or template parlays (e.g., “Shots on Goal Ladder”) - Bulk refresh and compare current payout vs. saved snapshot - Auto-notify on significant payout change thresholds Empty and error states - Empty slip: “Build your winning line—start from Live Now or Quick Builder” - Offline: frost overlay with “Signal in the penalty box—retry” and Retry button - Odds unavailable: greyed chips with “Temporarily off the board” Settings and preferences - Odds display: American/Decimal/Fractional - Sounds: On/Off; volume respect - Haptics: On/Off - Theme: Light, Dark, System - Data saver: lower image/animation fidelity on cellular Performance and tech notes - Lazy-load team assets; prefetch logos for upcoming games - Virtualized lists for large slates - Use Lottie or lightweight vector animations for pulses and rink spinner - Safe areas respected; bottom-sheet slip clears keyboard when entering stake Microcopy examples - Place Bet CTA: “Place EQ12 Parlay” - Odds change dialog: “Price changed from +480 to +460. Accept and place?” - Save confirmation: “Parlay saved to Favorites” - Notifications toggle: “Get goal and odds alerts” Responsible gaming - Persistent “RG” icon linking to limits and support - Nudges for time-on-app and spending reminders (configurable) QA checklist - One-handed reachability for all primary CTAs - Swipe gestures discoverable (first-run coach marks with visual hints) - Live odds animation doesn’t cause layout shift - VoiceOver reads odds changes and payouts succinctly This design delivers a premium, hockey-first betting experience: fast, tactile, visually rich, and built for live action with swipeable cards, animated odds, a frictionless quick builder, and robust favorites and notifications—wrapped in a distinctive NHL rink aesthetic that feels right at home on mobile.
