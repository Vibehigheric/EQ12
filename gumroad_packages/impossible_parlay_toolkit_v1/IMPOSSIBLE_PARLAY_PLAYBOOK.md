## Chapter 2: The Mathematics of Beautiful Failure

### Understanding Compound Probability

When you combine multiple bets, probabilities multiply:

```
Single bet: 50% chance (1 in 2)
2-leg parlay: 25% chance (1 in 4)  
6-leg parlay: 1.6% chance (1 in 64)
20-leg parlay: 0.000036% chance (1 in 2.7 million)
```

5. **Responsible Messaging**: Promote mathematical understanding
